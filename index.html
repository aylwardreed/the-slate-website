Privacy & Terms

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to The Slate app (hereby referred to as “Application”) for iOS devices, created by Jonathan Reed (hereby referred to as “Service Provider”). This service is intended for use “AS IS”.

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not obtain any personal information when you download and use it. The only data collected is high-level App Store metrics, such as number of installs, daily active users, subscription numbers, etc. None of this is personally identifiable.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

No. The Service Provider is committed to high levels of privacy. No data is collected or shared with third parties.

Does the Service Provider know which talent I’m tracking?

No. All data regarding who you track, what you search for, which projects you view, your settings, and more is stored locally on your device.

What data DOES travel off the device?

Your list of favourites and settings is stored remotely and securely in iCloud – if you enable the setting. To opt out of this, make sure the function is turned off in settings. Additionally, your search queries are sent securely to the TMDB API, which returns search results to you. Only the search term (such as ‘Edgar Wright’) travels off-device and is not identifiable to you.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. However, since the Application does not collect any information, there is no risk of your data being accessed by unauthorised individuals.
